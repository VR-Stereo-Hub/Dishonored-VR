# switch table. Each row flips or sets one switch through the mod's `console` seam word and puts
# it back; nothing is written to any ini. Whether the command is reached through that route is
# itself what the first rows show: a row that changes nothing in the picture or the numbers,
# while `stages` shows its stage still running, means the route does not reach the handler.
